About VMEN Power Technology

Reliable energy and backup-power solutions for homes, businesses and industrial operations across Zambia.

Who We Are

Energy & Power Technology

VMEN Power Technology Energy Company Limited operates in the energy and power technology space, providing generators, solar panels, lithium batteries, inverters and related generator products and services. We are based at Munali Roundabout, Great East Road, Lusaka, Zambia.

Our generator range spans approximately 10kVA up to 1250kVA, covering power requirements from smaller residential and business needs through to demanding commercial and industrial environments.
